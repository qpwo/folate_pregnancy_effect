NOTES
=====
- The MRC Vitamin Study (1991) is the most important missing paper. It's the landmark RCT that established folic acid supplementation for prevention of neural tube defects. It's paywalled on the Lancet website and not available on PMC, Europe PMC, or Anna's Archive.
- Berry 1999 (NEJM) is the second most important missing paper. It's the China-US Collaborative Project that confirmed folic acid prevents NTDs. It's paywalled on NEJM.
- For creatine, there are very few RCTs in pregnancy. The field is still in early stages with mostly preclinical/animal studies and one PK trial (Naidu 2025). No actual RCTs of creatine supplementation in pregnancy have been published yet.
- The previous session did extensive work downloading 76 folate PDFs from PubMed and creating markdown summaries.
